Security & Privacy / 安全與隱私

  • No passwords: We never ask for your TikTok password. Authorization is done via OAuth 2.0.
  • Minimal access: Access is limited to what is necessary for video upload (per the scopes you approve).
  • No selling data: We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • Revoke anytime: You can revoke authorization from TikTok settings at any time.
